Privacy Policy — Perio360

Last updated: [confirm date before publishing]

This policy describes how Perio360 ("the app"), developed by Dr. Mohammad Abu-Samak Dental Inc., handles information when you use it.

Information the app processes

Perio360 is a clinical decision-support tool for periodontal and peri-implant classification. When you use the auto-diagnosis feature, you may upload a periodontal chart (PDF or image). That file is converted to an image and sent to Google's Gemini Vision API to extract clinical measurements (probing depths, clinical attachment loss, bleeding on probing, mobility, furcation involvement) used to propose a stage and grade, which you then review and confirm.

Third-party processing

Chart images submitted through the auto-diagnosis feature are processed by Google's Gemini API, subject to Google's own privacy and data-handling terms. No chart data is sold or used for advertising.

Data you enter manually

Periodontal, other-condition, and peri-implant diagnosis inputs entered directly in the app are used only to generate your session's staging/grading summary and report.

Perio360 is intended for use by dental professionals as a decision-support aid, not a substitute for clinical judgment. Do not upload charts containing identifiable patient information unless your use of the app is consistent with your jurisdiction's privacy and health-information regulations.
