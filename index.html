Mohammad Abu-Samak, BDS, PhD, MPerio (Candidate)

Graduate Periodontics resident, Dalhousie University. PhD, Oral Health Sciences, McGill University. Clinical and research focus on periodontics, peri-implant diseases, and chronotherapy — the timing of therapeutic interventions to improve treatment outcomes.

Research

Research program spans chronotherapy trials (NSAID timing, post-surgical pain), periodontal epidemiology (NHANES-based cohort studies), finite element analysis of implant biomechanics, and the development of digital diagnostic tools for periodontal classification.

Perio360

Perio360 is a clinical decision-support tool implementing the 2017 AAP/EFP World Workshop classification of periodontal and peri-implant diseases. It walks a clinician through periodontal diagnosis, other conditions, and peri-implant diagnosis, and produces a summary staging and grading report.

The iOS app includes an auto-diagnosis feature: a clinician can upload a periodontal chart, and the app extracts probing depths, clinical attachment loss, bleeding on probing, mobility, and furcation involvement per tooth to propose a stage and grade, which the clinician then confirms.
